buildingSMART Norway's work for sustainability and circularity

buildingSMART Norway's mission is to improve the digital flow of information in the construction process. We participate in the development of the open international buildingSMART standards and work to ensure that they are implemented in software and utilized in the Norwegian construction industry and public administration.

Open standards for the entire building's lifecycle

These issues are central to the work with the UN's sustainable development goal 11 which concerns cities and communities. buildingSMART Norway aligns with the recommendations from Bygg21, and supports the use of enriched BIM through all phases of the construction process, and also the creation of a digital twin for construction and to be used in the building's operational phase and during renovation. Being able to digitally track, verify and document properties in individual components of buildings is central when we build the cities and communities of the future. It creates transparency and open standards ensure lasting verifiability throughout the building's lifespan.

The key to a sustainable construction and real estate industry and the transition to circular economy is that development is based on open standards and machine-readable data. Therefore, buildingSMART Norway is working to adopt open standards and create digital collaboration from a lifespan perspective among actors throughout the value chain. This ties into the work with the UN's sustainable development goal 9 on industry, innovation, and infrastructure, where a central component is technology as a basis for the underlying infrastructure.

A Norwegian ecosystem

buildingSMART organises various activities in the professional network and holds open webinars on relevant topics. We wish to be part of creating a dynamic Norwegian ecosystem where we highlight best practices from our members and partners for the benefit of the industry as a whole.

Collaboration is the key

The UN Sustainable Development Goal 17 focuses on collaboration to achieve the goals that are central. For buildingSMART Norway, it is particularly important during the period 2020/2021 to strengthen collaboration with relevant organizations in the industry to stand together in promoting sustainable development. As part of buildingSMART International, we are involved in influencing the development of open standards at a global level, and we are contributing to raising sustainability on the agenda within the organization.

buildingSMART Norway collaborates with a number of organizations to ensure sustainable development in the construction industry. Our goal for the coming years is to continue to cooperate with these actors and create common relevant activities that drive change. The cooperation agreements should be anchored and be concrete, measurable, and accessible.

Internal measures in the administration:

buildingSMART Norway's administration is aware of our impact and responsibility towards the environment, climate, and society, and therefore it is important for us to set a good example and stand as a responsible and serious player for our members.

  • Our employees are offered a public transport and city bike subscription. It is recommended to travel by public transport or cycle whenever possible;

  • We avoid flying and choose the train whenever possible. We purchase environmental credits whenever it is possible.

  • We provide the opportunity to participate digitally in member meetings and network meetings;

  • We are located in a shared office space with access to a variety of shared services and an Environmentally Certified conference centre.

  • Our employees are offered an agreement for remote work.

  • Our office furniture is purchased used.

  • When we replace IT equipment and office furniture, it is donated to students, sold, or given away.
