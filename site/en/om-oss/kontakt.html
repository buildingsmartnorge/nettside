The administration

The administration of buildingSMART Norway is responsible for the daily operations of the association. We follow up on members, coordinate professional activities and events, and support the domains and working groups within the network.

The board

The board of buildingSMART Norway is the association's highest governing body between the annual meetings. The board follows up on the adopted goals and priorities and is responsible for the strategic direction, finances, and overall management of the association's activities.

Nomination committee

The Nomination committee is responsible for preparing elections for the board of buildingSMART Norway. The committee collects proposals and assesses candidates, and presents its recommendation at the annual meeting, which formally elects the board.
